I am looking for motivated and talented students to join my research group.
If you are interested in pursuing graduate studies, and in particular a PhD, in the areas of computer networks, I would be happy to hear from you.
The research in my lab focuses on a variety of topics in the area of computer networks and distributed systems.
I am especially interested in network performance measurement and analysis, the interaction of emerging network applications with wireless networks, and modern programmable network infrastructure (e.g., programmable switches and SmartNICs).
If you are passionate about these topics and eager to contribute to cutting-edge research, please apply!

As a PhD student in my group, you will have the opportunity to work on exciting research projects, collaborate with other researchers, and contribute to the advancement of knowledge in networks.
You will be part of a small, supportive group of technical people who are dedicated to deeply understand technology and solve challenging problems through rigorous research, experimentation, and system building.

## Requirements

Candidates should have a strong foundation in computer science, especially in computer networks, distributed systems, or closely related areas. 
Proficiency in programming languages such as C, C++, or Python is expected. 
Experience with network programming, network protocols, wireless networks, and network measurement tools is highly desirable but not required.
Applicants with practical project or research experience with published papers in these areas are especially encouraged to apply.

## Funding

Funding will be awarded competitively to applicants who demonstrate strong preparation—through technical expertise, research or professional experience, prior publications, or contributions to open-source projects.
The support includes full tuition coverage and a stipend, and is open to both domestic and international candidates.
Additionally, the funding package covers research equipment and conference travel expenses.

## Application Process

To be considered for this position and for funding, you must first gain admission to Illinois Tech’s PhD program in Computer Science. 
Admission decisions are made by a departmental committee that reviews all applicants holistically across multiple criteria.
You may apply even if your master’s degree is in a field other than Computer Science.
Once admitted and if you’ve indicated interest in pursuing your PhD under my supervision you will then be considered for a funded position.
To apply, visit the [PhD program webpage](https://www.iit.edu/academics/programs/computer-science-phd) and apply there.

Please make sure to review the official application deadlines for both [domestic](https://www.iit.edu/admissions-aid/graduate-admission/domestic-students/admission-dates-and-deadlines) and [international](https://www.iit.edu/admissions-aid/graduate-admission/international-students/admission-dates-and-deadlines) applicants.
Since the PhD application process can take time, especially as it requires collecting materials such as recommendation letters, it makes sense to begin early to avoid any last-minute issues or unexpected delays.

## About Illinois Tech

The Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech) is a leading research university located in Chicago, known for its strong emphasis on innovation, technology, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
Within the university, the Department of Computer Science has built a distinguished reputation for excellence in research and education, attracting world-class scholars whose work spans areas such as artificial intelligence, systems, and cybersecurity.
The department fosters a vibrant academic environment that combines rigorous theoretical foundations with impactful applied research, contributing to advances that shape both academia and industry.

![Chicago](/assets/img/chicago.jpg "Chicago")

Illinois Tech is located just a few minutes south of downtown [Chicago](https://www.iit.edu/student-experience/life-in-chicago), offering students easy access to one of the most dynamic urban centers in the United States.
Our campus is easily reachable via two subway lines and multiple bus routes, making commuting convenient for students living in various parts of the city.
Its proximity to the city’s thriving technology, finance, and healthcare industries creates abundant opportunities for internships, collaborations, and career growth.
Beyond professional prospects, Chicago provides an exceptional quality of life, cultural institutions, sports, concerts, lakefront parks, and diverse neighborhoods, making it an ideal setting for both academic pursuits and everyday living.
